🛡️ JobShield – AI-Powered Recruitment Fraud Detection

Empowering Every Job Seeker to Identify Risks Before Trusting Opportunities.

📌 About the Project

JobShield is an AI-powered platform designed to detect fake job offers, recruitment scams, and fraudulent hiring activities.

It analyzes job advertisements, messages, URLs, company information, and payment requests to identify suspicious recruitment activities and help job seekers make safer decisions.

🎯 Problem Statement

- Fake job offers are spreading through WhatsApp, Telegram, emails, and websites.
- Fraudsters demand registration and interview fees.
- Job seekers are exposed to financial fraud and identity theft.
- Freshers and students struggle to identify genuine job opportunities.

💡 Proposed Solution

JobShield uses Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to identify potential recruitment fraud and provide understandable risk assessments.

🚀 Key Features

1. AI Job Analyzer – Detects suspicious language in job advertisements and messages.
2. URL Safety Checker – Analyzes suspicious recruitment links.
3. Company Verification – Checks company identity and contact information.
4. Payment Scam Detector – Identifies suspicious registration and interview fee requests.
5. Explainable AI – Provides reasons behind risk assessments.
6. Community Fraud Intelligence – Uses user reports to identify recurring scam patterns.

🔄 Working Process

1. User submits a job advertisement, message, email, screenshot, or URL.
2. System extracts relevant information.
3. NLP analyzes suspicious language.
4. Machine Learning identifies fraud patterns.
5. URL reputation is checked.
6. Company information is verified.
7. Risk is classified as Low, Medium, or High.
8. Explainable AI displays suspicious indicators.
9. Users can report suspicious offers.
10. Community intelligence helps identify recurring scams.

🌍 Expected Impact

- Helps protect students and fresh graduates.
- Raises awareness about recruitment fraud.
- Reduces exposure to job-related scams.
- Supports safer and more informed job decisions.
- Promotes a safer recruitment ecosystem.
